# Creating a device group

Device groups let you organise devices by site, customer or purpose, so you can filter telemetry and apply notification rules to several devices at once.

## Create the group

Go to **Devices -> Device groups** and click **Add group**. Give it a name and an optional description, then save.

Names only need to be unique within your organisation, so two organisations can both have a group called "Main Site".

## Add devices to the group

Open the group and click **Manage devices**. Tick the devices you want and save.

A device can belong to more than one group. Removing a device from a group does not delete the device or any of its readings.

## Using the group

Once devices are grouped, the group appears as a filter option on the live data and readings pages, and can be selected as the target when setting up notification rules.
